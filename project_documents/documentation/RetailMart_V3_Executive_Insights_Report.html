5. Scenario Simulator Levers & Executive Action Directives

Validated baseline parameters and strategic tactical mandates for the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO).

1. Corporate Overhead Cost Rationalization (CFO Mandate)

Audit top 3 corporate expense lines (IT Infrastructure, Marketing Campaigns, Consulting/Legal fees) accounting for ₹420.1 Cr (52.4% of total non-store spend). Enact zero-based budgeting to eliminate discretionary leakage.

Priority: P0 - Immediate | Deadline: Q2 2026 | Owner: CFO

2. Dynamic Treasury Pooling & Payroll Liquidity Buffer (CFO Mandate)

Consolidate bank account balances across the 200 corporate accounts to maintain at least 1.5x monthly contractual payroll coverage (₹28.5 Cr) via automated liquidity sweeps, preventing cash-flow crunches.

Priority: P0 - Immediate | Deadline: 30 Days | Owner: CFO

3. Retail Branch Staffing Rebalancing (CHRO Mandate)

Redeploy store staff from underperforming branches (<₹15L revenue/employee) to high-volume metropolitan stores (>₹30L revenue/employee) to optimize labor-to-revenue ratio and maximize store contribution margin.

Priority: P1 - High | Deadline: 60 Days | Owner: CHRO

4. Store Manager Contribution Incentive Plan (CHRO Mandate)

Re-align retail store manager annual incentive structures from gross revenue quotas to store operating contribution margin (Delivered Sales - COGS - Store Expenses - Store Payroll).

Priority: P2 - Medium | Deadline: Q3 2026 | Owner: CHRO
