# RetailMart V3 - Comprehensive KPI Catalogue
## Authoritative Metric Specification Register

This catalogue defines all approved KPIs across the **Executive Summary**, **Sales**, **Customer**, **Operations**, and **Cross-Functional** dashboard domains in accordance with enterprise BI governance rules.

---

### KPI 1: Delivered Net Revenue
- **Domain**: Executive Summary & Sales Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Recognized monetary net revenue from commercial purchase orders successfully delivered to customers.
- **Business Purpose**: Core commercial performance indicator reflecting realized enterprise top-line income.
- **Formula**: `SUM(o.net_total)`
- **Grain**: Order Grain (`sales.orders.order_id`)
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.orders.net_total`, `sales.orders.order_status`
- **Join Path**: Direct table query (or joined to `core.dim_date` on `order_date = date_key`)
- **Date Basis**: `sales.orders.order_date`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: `WHERE order_status = 'Delivered'` (Excludes `Cancelled`, `Failed`, `Returned`, `Processing`, `Out for Delivery`, `Shipped`)
- **Feasibility**: **Directly Available**
- **Limitations**: Excludes pipeline revenue currently in transit.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Primary Executive KPI Card with sparkline; drill-down to Monthly Trend and Category Split.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT SUM(net_total) FROM sales.orders WHERE order_status = 'Delivered';` (Reconciles to ₹6,769,536,004.48 / ₹676.95 Cr).
- **Management Action**: If actual revenue deviates $>5\%$ below comparable prior period, trigger regional demand acceleration and promotional review.

### KPI 2: Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Domain**: Executive Summary & Sales Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Average net revenue realized per completed purchase order.
- **Business Purpose**: Measures purchasing basket size efficiency and pricing realization.
- **Formula**: `ROUND(SUM(net_total)::numeric / COUNT(order_id), 2)`
- **Grain**: Order Grain
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.orders.net_total`, `sales.orders.order_id`
- **Join Path**: Direct table query
- **Date Basis**: `sales.orders.order_date`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: `WHERE order_status = 'Delivered'`
- **Feasibility**: **Derivable**
- **Limitations**: Averages can mask bimodal distributions (e.g., small bulk items vs high-end electronics).
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: KPI card; category and payment mode cross-tabulations.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT ROUND(SUM(net_total) / COUNT(*), 2) FROM sales.orders WHERE order_status = 'Delivered';` (Reconciles to ₹82,015.22).
- **Management Action**: Upsell bundling and minimum order thresholds if AOV slips.

### KPI 3: Commercial Order Volume
- **Domain**: Executive Summary & Sales Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Total count of purchase orders generated by customers, segmented by fulfillment status.
- **Business Purpose**: Monitors order throughput and transaction velocity across channels.
- **Formula**: `COUNT(order_id)`
- **Grain**: Order Grain
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.orders.order_id`, `sales.orders.order_status`
- **Join Path**: Direct table query
- **Date Basis**: `sales.orders.order_date`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: Evaluated for all orders (150,000 total) and Delivered orders (82,540).
- **Feasibility**: **Directly Available**
- **Limitations**: Does not distinguish between single-item and multi-item orders without joining line items.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Donut chart of orders by status; daily/weekly bar chart.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT order_status, count(*) FROM sales.orders GROUP BY order_status;`
- **Management Action**: Investigate spike in `Cancelled` ($>15\%$) or `Failed` orders ($>5\%$) with payment gateway and logistics teams.

### KPI 4: Active Customer Base
- **Domain**: Executive Summary & Customer Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Number of unique customers who completed at least one delivered purchase in the period.
- **Business Purpose**: Quantifies active customer demand and active user penetration.
- **Formula**: `COUNT(DISTINCT cust_id)`
- **Grain**: Customer Grain (`customers.customers.customer_id`)
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.orders.cust_id`, `sales.orders.order_status`
- **Join Path**: `sales.orders` $\bowtie$ `customers.customers` on `cust_id = customer_id`
- **Date Basis**: `sales.orders.order_date`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: `WHERE order_status = 'Delivered'`
- **Feasibility**: **Derivable**
- **Limitations**: Does not account for registered accounts that only browsed without purchasing.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Trend line of monthly active buyers vs new customer registrations.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT cust_id) FROM sales.orders WHERE order_status = 'Delivered';` (Reconciles to 40,380 unique customers).
- **Management Action**: Trigger re-engagement campaigns for inactive registered customers.

### KPI 5: Customer RFM Segment Distribution
- **Domain**: Customer Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Categorization of customers into behavioural tiers based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scores.
- **Business Purpose**: Guides retention, VIP loyalty rewards, and targeted win-back promotions.
- **Formula**: `NTILE(5)` rank over recency days, order count, and net spend.
- **Grain**: Customer Grain
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.orders.cust_id`, `sales.orders.order_date`, `sales.orders.net_total`
- **Join Path**: Aggregated `sales.orders` per customer
- **Date Basis**: `order_date` relative to anchor date `2026-02-26`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: `WHERE order_status = 'Delivered'`
- **Feasibility**: **Derivable**
- **Limitations**: Static quantile boundaries shift as customer tenure expands.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Bubble chart (Recency × Frequency with Monetary bubble size); actionable customer table.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT rfm_segment, count(*) FROM analytics.vw_customer_rfm_scores GROUP BY rfm_segment;`
- **Management Action**: Allocate VIP perks to 'Champions' and personalized discounts to 'At Risk' customers.

### KPI 6: Courier On-Time Delivery (OTD) SLA Rate
- **Domain**: Executive Summary & Operations Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Percentage of delivered outbound shipments completed within the standard SLA window ($\le 5$ transit days).
- **Business Purpose**: Core logistics carrier performance KPI measuring courier reliability.
- **Formula**: `COUNT(CASE WHEN delivered_date - shipped_date <= 5 THEN 1 END)::numeric / COUNT(delivered_date)::numeric * 100`
- **Grain**: Parcel Shipment Grain (`sales.shipments.shipment_id`)
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.shipments.shipped_date`, `sales.shipments.delivered_date`, `sales.shipments.courier_name`
- **Join Path**: Direct table query on `sales.shipments`
- **Date Basis**: `sales.shipments.shipped_date`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: `WHERE status = 'Delivered' AND delivered_date IS NOT NULL`
- **Feasibility**: **Derivable**
- **Limitations**: Excludes parcels lost in transit or returned prior to initial delivery attempt.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Stacked horizontal bar chart by courier; transit time distribution.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT courier_name, ROUND(AVG(delivered_date - shipped_date), 2) FROM sales.shipments WHERE status = 'Delivered' GROUP BY courier_name;`
- **Management Action**: Reallocate parcel dispatch volumes away from couriers breaching the 90% SLA threshold.

### KPI 7: Store Inventory Stock-Out Risk SKUs
- **Domain**: Executive Summary & Operations Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Count and proportion of retail store SKUs whose stock on hand is at zero or below defined reorder thresholds.
- **Business Purpose**: Prevents lost store sales and stockouts across retail branches.
- **Formula**: `COUNT(DISTINCT product_id) WHERE quantity_on_hand <= reorder_level`
- **Grain**: Store SKU Inventory Grain (`store_id` × `product_id`)
- **Tables & Columns**: `products.inventory.quantity_on_hand`, `products.inventory.reorder_level`
- **Join Path**: `products.inventory` $\bowtie$ `stores.stores` on `store_id`
- **Date Basis**: Current snapshot state
- **Filters & Exclusions**: Excludes discontinued products if inactive flag exists.
- **Feasibility**: **Directly Available**
- **Limitations**: Represents store shelf stock; does not measure warehouse replenishment pipeline without joining inbound shipments.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Heatmap of low stock SKUs by Store & Category.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM products.inventory WHERE quantity_on_hand <= reorder_level;` (Reconciles to 32,593 store-SKU combinations).
- **Management Action**: Trigger automated warehouse dispatch for stores in critical stock-out status.

### KPI 8: Factory Manufacturing Rejection Rate
- **Domain**: Operations Dashboard
- **Business Definition**: Percentage of manufactured units rejected during factory production quality control runs.
- **Business Purpose**: Monitors production quality, scrap costs, and line equipment reliability.
- **Formula**: `ROUND(SUM(rejected_quantity)::numeric / NULLIF(SUM(quantity_produced), 0) * 100, 2)`
- **Grain**: Work Order Grain (`manufacture.work_orders.work_order_id`)
- **Tables & Columns**: `manufacture.work_orders.quantity_produced`, `manufacture.work_orders.rejected_quantity`
- **Join Path**: `manufacture.work_orders` $\bowtie$ `manufacture.production_lines` on `line_id`
- **Date Basis**: `manufacture.work_orders.start_timestamp`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: `WHERE status = 'Completed'`
- **Feasibility**: **Derivable**
- **Limitations**: Does not capture component-level raw material defect causality.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Bar chart of rejection rate by production line and supervisor.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT ROUND(SUM(rejected_quantity)::numeric / SUM(quantity_produced) * 100, 2) FROM manufacture.work_orders WHERE status = 'Completed';`
- **Management Action**: Schedule machine calibration for assembly lines with scrap rates exceeding 3.5%.

### KPI 9: Return Cost Impact & Return Rate
- **Domain**: Cross-Functional Dashboard & Sales
- **Business Definition**: Total financial value of refunds issued and percentage of delivered orders returned by customers.
- **Business Purpose**: Quantifies revenue erosion and product quality / customer satisfaction failures.
- **Formula**: Return Rate: `COUNT(DISTINCT r.order_id)::numeric / COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id) * 100`; Refund Impact: `SUM(r.refund_amount)`
- **Grain**: Return Item Grain (`sales.returns.return_id`)
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.returns.refund_amount`, `sales.returns.reason`, `sales.orders.order_id`
- **Join Path**: `sales.orders` $\bowtie$ `sales.returns` on `order_id`
- **Date Basis**: `sales.returns.return_date`
- **Filters & Exclusions**: Only valid processed return claims.
- **Feasibility**: **Derivable**
- **Limitations**: Does not include return reverse logistics courier freight costs.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Treemap of return reasons; refund cost impact by product category.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT order_id), SUM(refund_amount) FROM sales.returns;` (Reconciles to 7,465 returned orders / ₹297,716,634.62).
- **Management Action**: Review vendor quality for SKUs with $>10\%$ return rates driven by 'Defective' or 'Size issue'.

### KPI 10: Revenue at Risk (Proxy Metric)
- **Domain**: Cross-Functional Dashboard & Executive Summary
- **Business Definition**: Estimated gross revenue jeopardized by operational failures (cancellations + failed orders) and churn risk from previously high-spending customers.
- **Business Purpose**: Provides proactive management visibility into addressable commercial leakage.
- **Formula**: `SUM(net_total WHERE order_status IN ('Cancelled', 'Failed')) + SUM(monetary_value of At-Risk Champions)`
- **Grain**: Composite Metric
- **Tables & Columns**: `sales.orders.net_total`, `sales.orders.order_status`, `analytics.vw_customer_rfm_scores`
- **Join Path**: Multi-domain aggregation
- **Date Basis**: Trailing 90-day window
- **Filters & Exclusions**: Clearly labeled as a **Proxy Metric**.
- **Feasibility**: **Proxy Metric**
- **Limitations**: Inactivity does not guarantee permanent loss; some churned customers may organically return.
- **Visual & Drill-Down**: Risk breakdown card; drill-through to high-priority at-risk customer list.
- **Validation Query**: `SELECT order_status, SUM(net_total) FROM sales.orders WHERE order_status IN ('Cancelled', 'Failed') GROUP BY order_status;`
- **Management Action**: Cross-functional taskforce between operations (to fix checkout/delivery failures) and retention marketing.
