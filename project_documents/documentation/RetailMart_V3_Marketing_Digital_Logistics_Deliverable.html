Authoritative Production SQL Queries

Parameterized PostgreSQL execution queries powering all KPIs and exploratory analyses.

SQL 1.1: Multi-Platform Ad Spend Allocation & Budget Utilization Grain: Platform Grain
Business Purpose: Aggregates total realized advertising expenditure, campaign count, and percentage share across all paid marketing channels.
SELECT 
    platform,
    COUNT(DISTINCT campaign_id) AS active_campaigns,
    ROUND(SUM(amount), 2) AS total_ad_spend,
    ROUND(SUM(amount) / SUM(SUM(amount)) OVER () * 100, 2) AS platform_share_pct
FROM marketing.ads_spend
GROUP BY platform
ORDER BY total_ad_spend DESC;
SQL 1.2: Email Engagement & Conversion Funnel Grain: Marketing Campaign Grain
Business Purpose: Measures total customer communication volume, email open rates, and click-through rates.
SELECT 
    SUM(emails_sent) AS total_sent,
    SUM(emails_opened) AS total_opened,
    SUM(emails_clicked) AS total_clicked,
    ROUND(SUM(emails_opened)::numeric / NULLIF(SUM(emails_sent), 0) * 100, 2) AS open_rate_pct,
    ROUND(SUM(emails_clicked)::numeric / NULLIF(SUM(emails_opened), 0) * 100, 2) AS ctr_pct
FROM marketing.email_clicks;
SQL 1.3: Digital Traffic & Device Ecosystem Distribution Grain: Device Type Grain
Business Purpose: Quantifies digital web footprint and mobile vs desktop traffic velocity.
SELECT 
    device_type,
    COUNT(view_id) AS page_views,
    COUNT(DISTINCT session_id) AS session_count,
    ROUND(COUNT(view_id)::numeric / SUM(COUNT(view_id)) OVER () * 100, 2) AS view_share_pct
FROM web_events.page_views
GROUP BY device_type
ORDER BY page_views DESC;
SQL 1.4: Outbound Carrier On-Time SLA & Transit Lead Times Grain: Courier Partner Grain
Business Purpose: Benchmarks logistics partners on fulfillment speed and SLA compliance.
SELECT 
    courier_name,
    COUNT(shipment_id) AS total_shipments,
    COUNT(CASE WHEN status = 'Delivered' THEN 1 END) AS delivered_parcels,
    ROUND(AVG(CASE WHEN status = 'Delivered' THEN delivered_date - shipped_date END)::numeric, 2) AS avg_lead_days,
    ROUND(
        COUNT(CASE WHEN status = 'Delivered' AND (delivered_date - shipped_date) <= 5 THEN 1 END)::numeric 
        / NULLIF(COUNT(CASE WHEN status = 'Delivered' THEN 1 END), 0) * 100, 
        2
    ) AS on_time_sla_pct
FROM sales.shipments
GROUP BY courier_name
ORDER BY total_shipments DESC;
