Authoritative Production SQL Queries

Parameterized PostgreSQL execution queries powering all KPIs and exploratory analyses.

SQL 3.1: Department Headcount & Monthly Payroll Allocation Grain: Department Master Grain
Business Purpose: Aggregates employee headcount, total base payroll, and average compensation across departments.
SELECT d.department_name, COUNT(e.employee_id) AS total_employees,
    ROUND(SUM(e.salary), 2) AS monthly_base_payroll,
    ROUND(AVG(e.salary), 2) AS avg_salary,
    ROUND(COUNT(e.employee_id)::numeric / SUM(COUNT(e.employee_id)) OVER () * 100, 2) AS headcount_share_pct
FROM stores.employees e
JOIN hr.dim_department d ON e.department_id = d.department_id
GROUP BY d.department_name ORDER BY total_employees DESC;
SQL 3.2: Corporate Overhead Expenditure by Category Grain: Corporate Expense Category Grain
Business Purpose: Reconciles top corporate non-payroll operating expenditures.
SELECT c.category_name, COUNT(e.expense_id) AS voucher_count,
    ROUND(SUM(e.amount) / 10000000.0, 2) AS total_crores,
    ROUND(SUM(e.amount) / SUM(SUM(e.amount)) OVER () * 100, 2) AS share_pct
FROM finance.expenses e
JOIN finance.dim_expense_category c ON e.category_id = c.category_id
GROUP BY c.category_name ORDER BY total_crores DESC LIMIT 8;
